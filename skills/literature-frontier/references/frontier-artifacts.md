# Frontier Artifacts

Use these templates as working records. Keep entries compact and replace stale conclusions rather
than appending contradictory prose.

## Research contract

```markdown
# Research contract
Question:
Target phenomenon/capability:
Objects and regime:
Progress criterion:
Comparison class:
Constraints:
Search current through:
Assumptions introduced by this framing:
```

## Search ledger

```markdown
| Date | Database/site | Query or traversal | Filters | Useful hits | New vocabulary | Coverage gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

Record failed queries when they tested an important synonym or scope. Do not record every trivial
keystroke.

## Claim evidence ledger

```markdown
| ID | Claim | Evidence source | Evidence type | Direct or inferred | Scope | Confidence | Disconfirming evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

One source may support only part of a sentence. Split composite claims until every row is auditable.

## Closest-work matrix

Choose technical columns that determine equivalence in the field.

```markdown
| Work | Problem/regime | Representation/state | Core operation | Objective/guarantee | Resource model | Evaluation | Failure boundary | Artifacts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

Mark incomparable evaluations explicitly. Do not rank methods by headline numbers measured on
different splits, budgets, or metrics.

## Contradiction and anomaly table

```markdown
| Observation | Sources in tension | Could differ because | Discriminating evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
```

Possible explanations include population, implementation, metric, compute, data leakage, time,
unstated assumptions, or genuine scientific disagreement.

## Opportunity ticket

```markdown

Unresolved claim or capability:

Evidence the delta is real:
Smallest decisive test:
Expected observation if the idea is right:
Strongest alternative explanation:
Kill criterion:
Novelty falsifier:
Resources needed:
Risk: technical | empirical | novelty | measurement | value
Current confidence:
```

## Final frontier brief

Lead with the evidence-backed answer to the research contract. Then include:

1. closest established results;
2. what comparisons are invalid or unresolved;
3. the ranked opportunity tickets;
4. novelty-search scope and date;
5. what new evidence would most change the ranking.

Avoid declaring an entire field exhausted or a gap definitely novel unless the search scope truly
supports that statement.
